{\bfseries Box 1.\ Structure of the two generated reviews.}

{\small
The pipeline produced one review per content track from the same corpus. Each
comprises 12 top-level sections --- an abstract, an introduction, nine
evidence-bearing sections, and conclusions --- subdivided into 45 (knowledge)
and 47 (frontier) subsections not listed here. The knowledge review runs to
17,975 words with 682 in-text citations; the frontier review to 16,391 words
with 612. Section titles and ordering were planned by the architect agent rather
than inherited from the theme hierarchy: six knowledge and five frontier macro
themes were regrouped into nine evidence-bearing sections each.
}
